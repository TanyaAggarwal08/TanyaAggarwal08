I'm a third-year Computing Science student who splits time between research and shipping software — and I keep landing on the same question in both: **where's the hidden structure in data that isn't supposed to have any?**

🏆 **Best Paper — IEEE 7th Annual World AIIoT Congress 2026**, for research applying persistent homology to detect socioeconomic boundaries and their link to crime concentration in urban data, with collaborators at TWU and SFU.

---

### 🔬 What I'm working on

**Finding boundaries with topology**
Persistent homology + frontage-weighted spatial statistics to detect land-use boundaries and how they relate to crime patterns — first in Vancouver, now extended to Austin.

**Recovering hidden physics under a glacier**
Research assistantship building a particle-filter data assimilation pipeline (Julia, `ParticleDA.jl`, `WAVI.jl`) to infer basal friction under an ice sheet from surface velocity data alone — the state you care about is never directly observable.

**Calm navigation for smart glasses**
Prototyping GPS breadcrumb navigation on Mentra Live smart glasses so elderly walkers get simple, reassuring guidance back home — plain language over numeric precision, by design.
